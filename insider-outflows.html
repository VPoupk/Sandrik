How to read this page. "Insider-linked" here means on-chain proximity to the deployer — a wallet that received a large block of AKE directly from the deployer or one hop away, before any market existed. That is a factual relationship, not proof of identity or intent. Where the evidence is strong we say so; where a wallet could plausibly be an arms-length OTC buyer, market-maker, or exchange, we flag it and lean against the "insider" reading. Confidence tags: high medium low / likely not insider.

Three Chains Funded Directly by the Deployer (Aug 16, 2025)

At launch the deployer sent 20.8B AKE (20.8% of supply) to three EOAs that are not labelled pools. None added this to a vesting contract; each forwarded it onward within days. This is the classic pattern for moving allocations off the deployer address before distribution/sale.

Chain A — the 13.8B EOA & the 11.65B Secondary Distributor high

EOA 0xa38da2eb received 13.8B directly from the deployer, then over Aug 17–25 2025 pushed it out in 16 transfers. 86% (11.65B) went to a single EOA, 0x07286aa1, which itself now holds zero — a second-layer distributor. The remaining ~1.9B was scattered to ~8 wallets (a market-maker-like wallet with 1,041 txns, several now-empty EOAs).

Chain C — 3B → the owner contract medium

Deployer → 0x432d8577 (3B, "LP EOA") → 0x681cf37b in one transfer (Aug 19 2025). Correction: the earlier report said this EOA "added 3B to PancakeSwap V3 liquidity" — on-chain it actually sent the full 3B to 0x681cf37b, the proxy contract that (a) holds 115 BNB and (b) was made owner of the AKE token on Feb 24 2026. Its own owner is 0x27b8a382 (an EOA, not the deployer).

So 3% of supply plus token ownership and a 115-BNB treasury sit behind one contract controlled by a separate operational key. Some of this 3B did ultimately seed PancakeSwap liquidity (the V3 pool holds 1.15B), but it routed through 0x681cf37b first rather than going to the pool directly.

Net read: The strongest insider signal is structural — 20.8B (≈21% of supply) left the deployer through unlabelled EOAs at launch, and the largest of these (11.65B via 0x07286aa1) was deliberately fanned out. Several individual recipients, however, behave like market-makers/exchanges rather than insiders; we have flagged those down rather than over-claiming.

Methodology & Coverage

Source: ERC-20 Transfer event logs (topic0 0xddf252ad…) from the AKE contract, filtered by sender address, pulled from an archival BSC node (onFinality). Balances/nonces/bytecode cross-checked on Binance public dataseed nodes.

"Received without buying": every row originates from a pool contract or a deployer-funded EOA — i.e. tokens granted by the team, not purchased on a market.

"Sold %": (total received − current balance) ÷ total received. ≥90% is treated as "sold/forwarded". This counts forwards-to-another-wallet the same as a market sale; both mean the original recipient no longer holds.

Coverage: Insider chains are reconstructed from complete Transfer-event logs for each EOA in the chain (these wallets have few transactions, so coverage is complete).

Block↔date: 0.571 s/block, anchored to deploy block 57,840,377 (Aug 16 2025). Dates ±~1 day.
