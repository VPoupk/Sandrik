Key findings

Price

NDT opened at the auction price of $0.30, spiked to $0.405 82 seconds in, and was at $0.060 at the snapshot, 80% below what bidders paid. In the first 3.2 hours, 750,698 NDT was sold into the pool for $128,858, and buyers took 332,999 NDT for $74,488.

Largest seller

One wallet, 0xb8ce…d091, sold 215,000 NDT in a single transaction (28.6% of all NDT sold) for $17,852. It got the tokens from the launch Safe's post-launch distribution, not from the auction, and sold them 2 min after they arrived. That one trade took the price from $0.116 to $0.061.

Auction bidders

189 of the 651 bidders sold (out of 391 who have claimed so far). Together they sold 322,762 NDT, which is 43.0% of all sells. 179 of them sold at least 95% of their bag, and the median wallet sold 3 min after claiming. 176 of the 189 sold at an average price below the $0.2999 they paid. These are mostly long-used wallets (146 were active more than a year before the auction) and read as individuals. The linked groups we found are small, mostly pairs of wallets with one owner.

Snipers

41 non-bidder wallets bought on the pool (22 of them within 90 seconds of it opening) and sold everything, 196,614 NDT (26.2% of sells). Several are multi-wallet bot clusters: same router, identical buy sizes, and exits in the same block. As a group they paid $61,720 and got back $57,650.

Airdrop

99 wallets have claimed the airdrop; 87 of them also bid in the auction. Claiming now pays out only 20%, and the rest goes back to the launch Safe. So far that returned 123,866 NDT to the Safe. Airdropped tokens make up about 3.5% of what was sold.

Overhang

The other 8 wallets from the Safe distribution still hold 1,267,000 NDT unlocked and untouched; 6 of them are addresses with no transaction history on any chain we checked. The launch Safe itself holds 1,415,037 liquid NDT. Nothing has left the vesting contract (3,383,000 NDT) yet.

The launch Safe distribution and the 215k dump

At mint, 2,950,167 NDT went straight to 0xe57a…60fd (a 3-of-6 Safe that also owns the airdrop contract and receives airdrop forfeits). At 18:06:15 UTC, 41 minutes after launch, it sent 1,482,000 NDT in one multisend 0x1dcc5b34… to nine wallets, and 177,000 NDT to the project treasury. None of these wallets took part in the auction.

Auction participants

Bidders who sold

The auction ran 16 Sep 15:10 to 30 Sep 16:00 UTC in USDC. Launch contract. Everyone paid the same $0.29985 per NDT; allocations were set off-chain in a merkle tree and were not pro-rata (153 of 391 claimers were filled in full, others got as little as 1% of their bid). The rest was refunded on claim, and 100% of claimed NDT was unlocked.

Selling was fast and complete. The median seller sold 3 min after claiming (74s at the 25th percentile, 8 min at the 75th). 119 of 189 sold within 5 minutes of their claim, and 179 sold 95% or more of what they held.

Most sold at a loss. 176 of 189 averaged below the $0.2999 auction price. Their auction tokens cost about $86,820 and brought back $46,596. The 202 claimers who have not sold still hold part of the 265,112 NDT left with claimers, and 260 bidders have not claimed at all.

Method and limits

  • Source data: every Transfer log of the NDT contract from the launch block to block 52,005,668, every event of the auction contract since its deployment, and the receipt of each NDT transaction (for USDC flows), all read from Base RPC. Contract names and wallet funding history come from Blockscout.
  • A sale is any transaction in which NDT moved into the Aerodrome pool (or the small Uniswap v4 pool). The seller is the wallet whose NDT balance fell in that transaction, so sales routed through LI.FI, Relay, 0x, 1inch, KyberSwap, OKX or bot routers are attributed to the real wallet. USDC received is the USDC that left the pool in that transaction.
  • Token source is followed through wallet-to-wallet transfers up to 4 hops. Bidders who also claimed the airdrop or bought extra are counted as bidders in the seller-type table and split proportionally in the origin line.
  • Wallet age uses the account nonce at fixed past blocks, so it measures the first transaction sent on Base. Smart-contract wallets (Coinbase Smart Wallet) can read younger than they are.
  • Clusters are evidence of common control, not proof. Shared funding from exchanges, bridges, routers and high-volume addresses is ignored. For every seller we pulled its 50 most recent incoming ETH and USDC transfers; for all 651 bidders we also pulled every USDC transfer they received from 30 days before the auction to its close. Older links can be missed, and bidders who never sold were not checked for ETH funding.
  • This is a snapshot 3.2 hours into trading. Claims and sales are still happening; 260 bidders and most airdrop recipients have not claimed yet.
