Executive summary

What this token is

VITARNA represents fractionalized intellectual-property rights to Artan Bio, a gene-therapy program (arginine-suppressor tRNA targeting nonsense mutations, lead candidate ARTAN-102, manufacturing with Lonza) funded on-chain through VitaDAO as IP-NFT #28. It is a DeSci research-funding asset, not a typical meme/utility coin — which shapes the entire holder map.

5.5M tokens were minted (5.0M at genesis in Mar 2024, +0.5M in Nov–Dec 2024). 20% of supply was sold to VITA holders via a crowdsale with a 12-month vesting lock — visible on-chain as the Sablier vesting NFTs and the StakedLockingCrowdSale contract.

    Method note — why the cluster count is small
    A naïve "everyone who received from the same address" graph would report one giant 95%+ cluster, because nearly all supply flows out of the genesis distributor and the DAO treasury. That is distribution, not common ownership. We therefore never link wallets through hubs (treasury, distributor, pools, routers, vesting, crowdsale, multisigs, or any wallet with >40 counterparties), and we discount shared funders that are high-throughput exchange/service wallets. What survives are genuine multi-wallet entities.

    07 Methodology & caveats

    Data

    • Full holder list (331) and complete transfer history (15,763 events, deploy → snapshot) pulled from the public Blockscout Ethereum API; balances reconstructed from transfers and validated against the authoritative holder endpoint (match within <0.01% on all but the treasury, off by 0.001%).
    • Address labels (ENS, contract names, protocol tags) from Blockscout; DEX pairs from Dexscreener; first-funder and out-degree from each wallet's native-ETH transaction history.
    • Pools: Uniswap V3 VITARNA/VITA & VITARNA/WETH, Uniswap V4 VITARNA/BIO (PoolManager singleton).
    • Cross-token overlap: exact VITA (0x81f8…F321) and BIO (0xcb15…5ffA) balances fetched per wallet via Blockscout; prices from Blockscout exchange rates at snapshot.

    How clusters are inferred

    • Transfer-peer: union-find over direct VITARNA transfers between non-hub wallets.
    • Common-funder: wallets first funded by the same low-throughput personal ETH wallet (out-degree < 40). Exchange/service funders are identified by out-degree (hundreds) and multi-million tx counts, and excluded.
    • Clustering proves linkage, not legal identity. A shared funder is strong but circumstantial; treat single-link clusters as leads, not proof.
