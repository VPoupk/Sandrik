Verdict — This List Does Not Carry the Insider Signature

On every test that identified the AKE insider cluster, the 47 buyback wallets come back clean. Not one of them is among the ten wallets that took 9.54bn out of the pools on 26 July. Not one appears among the 6,227 addresses that claimed the July node distribution. 28 of the 47 already held BNB before the AKE token contract existed. No gas funder is shared by more than one wallet on the list — the exact opposite of the ten July-26 wallets, which were all funded from one address in identical 0.0020 BNB amounts inside a single hour. Nonces run from 9 to 1,827; these are used wallets with histories, not burners.

Three weak links exist and are reported here for completeness, not because they carry weight. Three wallets were first funded from exchange hot wallets that also, at other times, funded AKE insider wallets. In every case the two fundings are months apart, and the funders are exchange withdrawal addresses that serve millions of unrelated users. This is the specific test — "funded from the same exchange as an AKE insider in the same time window" — and it fails to produce a match on the time-window half in all three cases.

How much weight this carries: very little. Binance 51, Binance Hot Wallet 9 and OKX's deposit/withdraw address process millions of withdrawals for unrelated users. A shared exchange funder is only meaningful when it comes with a tight time window, a repeated amount, or a shared destination — the combination that made the ten July-26 wallets conclusive. A fourth pattern worth noting is benign: two wallets on the list were gas-funded by two other wallets on the same list (0x14f96f80…d72b1c ← 0x34322f57…707e25, 0x7655d78d…e079ee ← 0x8625f346…ea8fb4), which is what you would expect from people running more than one wallet.

The node rewards these wallets received are small. Across all 47 wallets the project's pools paid out 24.858mn AKE in total, worth $19,903 at the prices on the days it was paid — an average of $423 per wallet. What They Spent, and What the Buyback Would Return

The node purchase is not visible on-chain from these wallets, and that is a finding rather than a gap in the scan. A full-history AKE trace and a USDT trace across the whole node-sale window (blocks 57,840,000 → 68,039,999, August 2025 to January 2026) show no payment of node-purchase size to any project contract. Total USDT moved out by all 47 wallets across that window is $31,645, and it is composed of DEX swap legs — the largest single flow is a $10,734 pass-through, routed via 1inch. There is no common recipient contract, no repeated ticket size, nothing shaped like a node sale.

The project did deploy seven "position" contracts (revert strings "Position does not exist", "user not match or already withdrawn") — but not until 4 December 2025, months after node rewards had already started flowing, and they carry about ten events each and hold zero AKE, zero USDT and zero BNB today. The conclusion the data supports is that ADODO nodes were paid for off-chain — fiat, an exchange account, or a payment rail that leaves no trace on BSC.

The asymmetry that matters. These wallets were paid their node rewards in AKE during August 2025 – April 2026, when AKE traded between $0.00019 and $0.0024. The buyback, if settled in AKE, is priced at today's $0.00454052 — the token is at or near its all-time high. The 24.858mn AKE the project has already paid this group was worth $19,903 when paid; the same tokens are worth $112,868 today. Whichever way "remaining value" is calculated, a buyback settled in AKE at today's price costs the project far fewer tokens than the same dollar promise would have cost at any point in the preceding twelve months.

Method, Coverage and Limitations

What was run

  • AKE transfer scan, topic-filtered on all 47 addresses in both directions, blocks 57,840,000 → 84,689,999 continuous, plus 85,600,000 → 91,449,999.
  • USDT (BSC-USD) transfer scan, same filter, blocks 57,840,000 → 68,039,999.
  • Recent-period pool coverage is complete from the verified pool reconciliation in the main report (blocks 100,940,328 → 113,384,906), which is how the zero-overlap with the July claimants is established.
  • Gas-funder trace for all 47: binary-search eth_getBalance to the first block each wallet held BNB, then read that block's transactions to find the crediting transfer. No explorer "Funded By" field was used.
  • Profiles: eth_getCode, eth_getTransactionCount, balanceOf at chain head.
  • Dating: 423-point block-timestamp grid; interpolation verified exact to the minute against four independently fetched blocks.
  • Pricing: CoinGecko daily series for akedo, applied per transfer date. The only flat price used is today's $0.00454052, and only where a present-day snapshot is explicitly being valued.

What this does not establish

  • Identity. Nothing here names anyone. The tests rule out the specific insider signature; they do not prove independence.
  • AKE coverage gap between blocks 84,690,000 and 85,600,000 and after 91,450,000 for non-pool counterparties. Pool-side flows in the recent period are covered separately and completely.
  • USDT after block 68,039,999 (January 2026 onward) was not scanned. A node purchase settled after that date would not appear.
  • Other rails. BNB, other stablecoins, other chains and off-chain payment were not traced. The absence of an on-chain node purchase is evidence about BSC AKE and USDT only.
  • The buyback terms are second-hand. No official document, contract or per-node price has been located.
  • A clean list is not a clean programme. These wallets look like ordinary node buyers. That says nothing about who set the buyback price, who funds it, or whether the ten July-26 wallets are connected to it.
