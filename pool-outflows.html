Headline: The eight pools are not self-service vesting contracts — every distribution is an admin-initiated push (adminTransfer / batch distribute) signed by the deployer. Recipients receive AKE without buying it. Of the 80 largest recipients traced across all pools + insider EOAs (who together received 18.4B AKE ≈ 18% of supply), 71 (88%) have since sold ≥90% of what they got. Only 6 are net accumulators (consistent with stakers). The main on-chain exit is the PancakeSwap V3 0.01% AKE/WBNB pool (0x4d3bf29b…, currently 1.15B AKE).

Two Distribution Archetypes

① Mass Airdrops — thousands of tiny wallets

CommunityPool and CreaterPlayerNodes 3 distribute in huge bursts of micro-transfers. In the captured early window alone, CommunityPool made 2,589 transfers to 2,241 wallets (median ≈ 3,400 AKE each); CreaterNodes 3 made 2,904 transfers (median ≈ 2,300 AKE). These look like reward/airdrop campaigns: recipients are retail wallets that received free tokens and, in aggregate, dumped them — adding steady sell pressure. A minority became net accumulators (stakers).

② Lump Grants — few large recipients

InvestorsPool, KOLPool, TeamAndAdvisors, CreaterNodes 1 & 2 send a small number of large transfers (millions–hundreds of millions of AKE) to specific wallets — investors, influencers, advisors. These are the highest-value "received-without-buying" wallets. As shown below, the overwhelming majority sold essentially everything.

TeamAndAdvisors 2 (0xd229b65d) is the exception: 15B AKE (15% of supply) has never moved — a standing overhang fully under admin control.

Methodology & Coverage

Source: ERC-20 Transfer event logs (topic0 0xddf252ad…) from the AKE contract, filtered by sender address, pulled from an archival BSC node (onFinality). Balances/nonces/bytecode cross-checked on Binance public dataseed nodes.

"Received without buying": every row originates from a pool contract or a deployer-funded EOA — i.e. tokens granted by the team, not purchased on a market.

Two "sold" measures: (1) Offload % = (received − current balance) ÷ received — counts any tokens that left the wallet, including forwards. (2) Exchange holding (the sell-pressure section) = AKE currently sitting at a verified spot exchange (Gate.io, KuCoin, Kraken, MEXC) or the PancakeSwap V3 DEX pool. Binance Alpha is excluded (airdrop platform, no AKE spot market). The first shows a wallet emptied; the second shows tokens at sell venues.

Coverage: Pool 'distributed' figures are exact (balance deltas). Recipient-level coverage: the early high-volume window (Aug–Sep 2025) and all large lump grants are captured via Transfer-event logs; the long tail of micro-airdrops is summarized statistically.

Block↔date: 0.571 s/block, anchored to deploy block 57,840,377 (Aug 16 2025). Dates ±~1 day.
