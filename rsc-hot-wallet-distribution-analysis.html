Three findings dominate. The payment population is small and highly skewed: the median payment is 850 RSC and 94% of all payments are under 10,000 RSC, yet 0.31% of transfers carry 36.1% of the value. Retention is low: 73.4% of recipients hold no RSC at all today, and only 13.9% of everything distributed still sits with the address it was paid to. And the largest non-bridge flows do not go to individuals at all — the two biggest destinations are both controlled by Endaoment, the US 501(c)(3) donor-advised-fund platform ResearchHub publicly partnered with in April 2025, which receives RSC as charitable donations and sells it on-chain to convert it to cash for university labs (§5).

It did not occur in isolation. On the same day, the Ethereum side of the hot wallet sent 1,200,000 RSC to the Base bridge — the second-largest transfer in its history. The 13 May payment was funded by a same-day cross-chain top-up rather than out of accumulated balance.

4.1 How much was sold

What can and cannot be measured

"Sold" is not directly observable on-chain. What is observable is that 15.99M RSC no longer sits at the address it was paid to, and where the next hop went for the largest recipients.

Tracing the 70 largest recipients — 13.91M RSC, 75% of everything distributed — gives ≥8.38M RSC sent directly to decentralised-exchange venues (CoW Protocol settlement, Aerodrome and Uniswap pools) and 129,965 RSC deposited directly to a centralised exchange. Of that DEX total, 5,971,610 RSC is Endaoment's liquidation programme (§5), measured from chain logs rather than an explorer index. The remainder of onward movement goes to addresses that are neither, which for the top recipients means intermediate hops of the kind documented in the on-chain memo's exchange-funnel section. A precise total sold is therefore not determinable; the defensible statement is that at least 8.5M RSC reached a trading venue in one hop, and that 86.1% of everything distributed has left the recipient address.

5. The largest destinations, identified

The two largest non-bridge destinations are not separate counterparties and are not ResearchHub-operated. Both are controlled by Endaoment, a US 501(c)(3) public charity that runs on-chain donor-advised funds. The third-largest is unrelated to them and terminates at a Coinbase-labelled address.

The identification, and how it is established

0x9d5025b3…d8b2 is ndao.eth. The ENS reverse record resolves to that name and the forward record for ndao.eth resolves back to the same address, so the pairing is confirmed in both directions rather than merely asserted by one side. 0xa41406f8…80b4c resolves the same way to admin.ndao.eth.

0xd90bea2b…4f8f is a Gnosis Safe v1.4.1 whose two owners are exactly those two addresses, with a threshold of 2. Reading getOwners() and getThreshold() on the Safe returns [0x9d5025b3…d8b2, 0xa41406f8…80b4c] and 2. The relationship between the two largest recipients is therefore ownership, not succession: one is a signer on the other.

ResearchHub and Endaoment announced this arrangement publicly on 30 April 2025. Endaoment provides the IRS-compliant infrastructure to receive RSC donations, convert them to fiat and transfer the proceeds to university research labs as cash gifts. The on-chain behaviour below matches that description in every respect.

5.2 How the RSC is disposed of

Every RSC that has entered the Endaoment complex has left it. Both accounts hold zero RSC today, and the two sides of the ledger balance exactly.

The disposal method is CoW Protocol, a batch-auction trading protocol: 5,971,609.54 RSC across 2,811 separate fills on 133 distinct days, from 27 May 2025 — seventeen days after the first donation — through 27 July 2026. The median fill is 1,654 RSC and the largest is 60,903 RSC. That is the profile of an automated, size-limited sell programme rather than block disposal.

These sales land in the same pools this analysis already measures

CoW routes orders to whatever venue offers the best price. Sampling 25 of the 2,811 fills and reading their transaction receipts, 24 contain a swap against one of the three tracked RSC pools — the two Aerodrome concentrated-liquidity pools and the Uniswap V3 1% pool. The Endaoment liquidation is therefore already inside the on-chain trading dataset, counted as taker sell-side flow.

The Coinbase round trip

One outflow did not go to a DEX. On 5 August 2025 the Safe sent 823,568.96 RSC to 0x3a236910…d1bd, which forwarded the identical amount to Coinbase 22 nine minutes later — the signature of a Coinbase deposit address being swept. Forty-one hours after that, Coinbase 22 sent 823,568.95 RSC back to the Safe, and the balance was subsequently sold through CoW like everything else. The round trip is unambiguous on-chain; whether it was a rejected deposit, a returned transfer or a deliberate withdrawal is not.

Decentralised-exchange net flow for RSC is −3,208,333 RSC year to date. Endaoment sold 3,362,102 RSC over the same period. Removing those sales and holding everything else constant turns the year's on-chain net flow slightly positive, at +153,769 RSC. In the 13 May – 30 June window the same adjustment takes net flow from −1,807,348 to −247,641.

This is a static attribution: it subtracts one participant's flow without modelling how other participants would have behaved in its absence. It does not claim the price would have been higher. What it establishes is that the year's on-chain selling pressure is not anonymous — it is a named charity converting donations to cash under a publicly announced arrangement, and it is large enough to account for the whole of the net figure.

5.4 The third-largest destination is not Endaoment

0x97e2ad35…c202f shares the surface characteristics of ndao.eth — an EOA carrying an EIP-7702 delegation — but the implementation it points at is a Coinbase smart-wallet contract (0x7702cb55…176c), not the MetaMask contract ndao.eth uses, and it has no ENS record. It received 1,459,882 RSC from the hot wallet in ten transfers between August and October 2025 and forwarded 1,459,902 RSC to 0x41377373…ef9c, which passed 1,459,358 RSC to Coinbase 22 in two transfers, retaining 544. Its identity is not established; its destination is.

5.5 A fourth pattern worth noting

0x4bdcf665…e68c received 867,017 RSC in a distinctly programmatic sequence: near-daily payments of 49,999.99, 74,999.99 and 99,999.98 RSC through late September and October 2025. Unlike the others, it still holds 866,265 RSC — essentially everything it was ever paid, making it the largest genuine holder among all hot-wallet recipients. Round-number amounts on a daily cadence, then no disposal, reads as a scheduled internal transfer or an escrow arrangement rather than a contributor payment. Searching for the address off-chain returns nothing, and it carries no ENS record or explorer name tag.

6. Links to other wallets in the RSC structure

Each of the 896 recipient-chain pairs was checked against the set of addresses identified elsewhere in this work — the treasuries, the multisig signers, the staking depositors and their funders, the exchange funnels, the vesting contracts and the labelled exchange wallets.

The two splits overlap — a monthly recipient can receive large payments — so they should not be added. What they establish jointly is that Treasury 3's external activity is dominated by a small, stable set of recurring payees rather than by a broad contributor population. 16 of its 83 external recipients also receive from the hot wallet, taking 5,911,292 RSC from Treasury 3 (41.0% of external flow), so the two payee populations overlap materially at the top even though their payment patterns differ.

What cannot be established here

RSC's USD price before August 2025 falls outside the range the public CoinGecko API serves, and the great majority of Treasury 3's payments predate that. These payments therefore cannot be sized in dollars, which means they cannot be compared against ResearchHub's published bounty values to confirm or exclude a bounty interpretation on amount alone. The classification above rests on cadence and recipient-set size, which are observable regardless of price.

Equally, a monthly schedule identifies a payment pattern, not a role. It is consistent with salaried staff, long-running editors, retained contractors or program grantees, and chain data does not distinguish between them.

8. Method and limits

  • Zero-value transfers are excluded throughout. 3,738 outbound transfers of exactly 0 RSC originate from address-poisoning activity by a third party rather than from ResearchHub, and their "recipients" are attacker-controlled look-alike addresses. Including them would inflate the recipient count by roughly 400 addresses.
  • The Base bridge and the burn address are excluded from the recipient population where stated. The bridge is the wallet moving its own funds between chains; the burn address is not a counterparty. Both are retained in the all-time distribution totals.
  • Retention is a point-in-time measure taken from current balances. It cannot distinguish a recipient who sold from one who moved funds to another wallet they control, and it does not capture RSC that was received, spent, and later re-acquired.
  • Onward-destination tracing covers the 70 largest recipients (13.91M RSC, 75% of distributed value), not the full population. Where an explorer index was used, the figure is a lower bound; the Endaoment totals in §5 are not — they were rebuilt directly from eth_getLogs over the full block range with no unrecovered chunks, and both accounts reconcile to a net of exactly zero against their live balanceOf. That rebuild raised the measured CoW Protocol total from 5,791,973 RSC in the explorer index to 5,971,610 RSC on chain.
  • Address roles are inferred from behaviour and contract type, except where an independent identifier exists. Two identifications in §5 do not rest on inference: ndao.eth and admin.ndao.eth are established by bidirectional ENS resolution plus Safe ownership, and Coinbase 22 by the public name tag on that address. Attempting the same off-chain search for 0x901Fc41a…480F, 0x4bdcf665…e68c, 0xB518536B…7d3d, 0x1288d4d0…8d34, 0x97e2ad35…c202f and the staking vault returned nothing indexed. An address is identifiable off-chain only when someone has published it; silence is not evidence either way.

All figures derive from complete outbound RSC transfer histories for 0x7F57d306a9422ee8175aDc25898B1b2EBF1010cb on Ethereum (1,340 transfer events) and Base (7,569 events), cross-checked between two independent explorer API routes and reconciled against balanceOf at checkpoints. Recipient balances were read directly from each chain at the time of writing and will drift. Amounts are in RSC; no USD conversion is applied in this document.
